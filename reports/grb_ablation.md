# GRB Dimension Ablation Study

## Objective

Measure how **readiness**, **profile rankings**, and **safeguard G1 activation** change when individual GRB dimensions are removed from the aggregation model (structural ablation on synthetic sensitivity profiles).

> Frozen GRB specification — synthetic profiles only; not municipal validation.

## Method

- **GRB version:** 0.1-experiment
- **Profiles:** 150 (deterministic sensitivity set)
- **Configurations:** `full`, `without_d2`, `without_d4`, `without_d6`
- **Full-model mean readiness (final):** 67.43
- **Full-model safeguard rate:** 22.7%
- **Safeguard G1:** cap at 60.0 when active D2 or D4 score < 2.0

### Ablation rules

| Configuration | Excluded | Active dimensions | Safeguard check |
|---------------|----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| `full` | none | d1–d6 | D2 and D4 |
| `without_d2` | D2 | d1, d3–d6 | D4 only |
| `without_d4` | D4 | d1–d3, d5–d6 | D2 only |
| `without_d6` | D6 | d1–d5 | D2 and D4 |

Indicator-level scores are unchanged; only the **dimension mean** entering overall maturity and applicable safeguards are ablated.

## Comparison vs full model

|---------------|------------------|----------|------------|-----------|--------------|-----------------------|
| `without_d2` | +2.03 | 3.61 | 0.8582 | 0.8403 | 130 | 34 → 6 (-28) |
| `without_d4` | +1.70 | 3.39 | 0.8575 | 0.8310 | 140 | 34 → 16 (-18) |
| `without_d6` | +1.83 | 2.63 | 0.9266 | 0.9617 | 140 | 34 → 12 (-22) |

## Interpretation notes

- Removing **D2** eliminates human-oversight maturity from the index and prevents D2 from triggering G1; profiles with weak D2 may show **higher** readiness under ablation.
- Removing **D4** removes data-legitimacy from the index and D4 safeguard triggers.
- Removing **D6** drops strategic-sovereignty from the mean only; safeguard behaviour matches the full model.
- Large rank displacement indicates sensitivity of **relative ordering** to dimension set choice.

## Limitations

- Uniform within-dimension scores per profile (sensitivity protocol).
- Ablation is a structural stress test, not a normative recommendation to omit dimensions.
- Does not re-run evidence gates item-by-item.
