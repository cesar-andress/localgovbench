## Forbidden claims

| # | Forbidden claim | Why | Safer wording |
|---|-----------------|-----|---------------|
| 1 | We measure **readiness** | Core reframing violation | We measure **public-evidence ceiling** |
| 2 | We **rank jurisdictions** | Hard exclusion | Sources are compared **descriptively**, not ranked |
| 3 | We **assess governance quality** | No outcome or dossier data | We map **evidence requirement satisfiability** |
| 4 | **Absent public evidence means absent governance** | Ecological fallacy | Public layer is **insufficient for gates**; internal evidence may exist |
| 5 | Public inventories are **bad or useless** | Normative overreach | Inventories provide **metadata and partial signals** |
| 6 | This **validates LocalGovBench as a readiness score** | Instrument not field-validated here | LocalGovBench supplies **evidence requirements** for ceiling test |
| 7 | Municipalities **fail** governance assessment | Implies scoring | Criteria are **gate-unreachable from public fields** |
| 8 | We measure **compliance** or **AI Act conformity** | Legal claim | Requirements **aligned with** policy themes; no legal verdict |
| 9 | **Observability** or **disclosure** of governance | Paper 2 / old framing | **Public-evidence ceiling** and **partial signal** |
| 10 | **Benchmark** performance of governments | Ranking language | **Multi-inventory satisfiability analysis** |
| 11 | **Maturity** or **maturity score** | Readiness synonym | **Evidence shortfall level** |
| 12 | Inventories **prove** lack of internal controls | Unobserved internal domain | Gates require **internal evidence floor** not visible in inventories |
| 13 | Vendor stewardship / procurement governance as **central finding** | Paper 2 firewall | Vendor fields noted as **weak proxies only** |
| 14 | **Documentary** or **genre** analysis | Paper 2 firewall | **Schema field mapping** only |
| 15 | **Validated instrument** (psychometric) | Delphi/dossiers pending | **Requirement catalogue** + ceiling study |

## Required disclaimer sentences (use ≥2 in abstract + discussion)

1. “**No readiness scores were assigned to programmes or jurisdictions.**”
2. “**Non-availability of public evidence does not imply absence of internal governance artefacts.**”
3. “Findings describe a **public-evidence ceiling**, not governance quality or legal compliance.”
